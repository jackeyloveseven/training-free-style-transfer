KEY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hybrid×2 achieves CSD 0.6442 in 6.40s — same quality as Adam×8
   (CSD 0.6097) but 3x faster.

2. SLT alone (α=0.1) costs nearly zero overhead (2.21s vs 2.20s baseline)
   but provides CSD 0.25 color pre-alignment, enabling Adam to converge
   in 2 iters instead of 8.

3. Ablation confirms each component is necessary:
   - Remove SLT → CSD drops 0.10 (Adam can't align color in 2 iters)
   - Remove Adam → CSD drops 0.39 (no brushstroke, only color shift)
   - Softmax vs Sinkhorn → CSD drops 0.05 (OT normalization matters)

4. Spatial smoothing proof: backward gradient neighbor cosine = 0.601,
   SLT update = 0.254, ratio 2.4x. Backward's attention Jacobian
   couples adjacent tokens → brushstroke; SLT is per-token independent.
